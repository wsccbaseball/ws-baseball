Advance Scout · Pitcher Book · SAMPLE PACKET

Riverside CC Staff

Wild, walk-prone, homer-prone — 6.85 ERA · 377 IP · 264 BB · 54 HBP · 39 WP · 55 HR · .276 BAA

The plan: make them throw strikes, ambush the fastball, and take what they give on the bases. Most of this staff unravels behind in the count — but the reads are arm-specific. Don't run one blanket approach across the whole staff.

By the Count

Get into hitter's counts and most of this staff goes 73–92% fastball with a ballooning slash — sit dead-red when ahead. Two arms break the pattern: Jake Mercer (#28) throws strikes behind (81%) and gets squared up rather than walking you — be ready to drive, not just take; and Lowe (#2) doesn't collapse at all — he pounds the zone for ground balls, so be aggressive early instead of waiting on walks that won't come.

Free Bases

264 BB + 54 HBP + 39 WP team-wide. Loudest arms: Ramsey (22 BB / 13.2 IP), Shaw (24 BB / 30 IP), Quinn (22 BB / 16.1 IP). Make them prove they can find the zone — take your walks, and once we're on, pressure them.
