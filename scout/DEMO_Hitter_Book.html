Riverside CC Lineup

34–17 grind-it-out, on-base machine — team .317 / .454 OBP / .475 SLG · 295 BB · 114 HBP · only 325 K · 40 HR · 27 SB (82%)

The plan: spin them down, keep the fastball out of the zone, and limit the damage when they reach — because they will reach. Win the at-bat with breaking stuff in the zone; don't give in to a fastball they can square.

Running Game — Our Job

RIV's offense runs only moderately — 27-for-33 (~82%) as a team, no burners (top thieves: Carter 6, Foster 4, Grant 4; Avery is just 3-for-6 — runs into outs). The steal is not their weapon; the walk and the single are. Hold the lead runner and vary times, but don't over-rotate to it — their bases clog on OBP, not speed.
